## Welcome

Welcome to my website! My name is Arthur Lamounier Moura, and I'm a fire ecologist interested in how fire and fire history affect forest structure, species composition, and tree regeneration. In addition to that, I investigate other disturbance processes, such as hurricane and lightning, and how they can influence forest dynamics and fire regimes. If you are interested in these topics, reach out to me! My contact information can be found below.
