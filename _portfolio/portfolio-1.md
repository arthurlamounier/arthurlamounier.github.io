In this project, we are investigating the distribution patterns of lightning strikes in a longleaf pine woodland by testing whether lightning strike events are randomly distributed clustered or dispersed. We also analyze a subset of lightning strikes with a high potential to cause wildfires (i.e., long-continuing-current) to develop a predictive risk model of lightning-driven ignitions and tree damage to support identification and mitigation of high-risk areas.
