--- title: "Effects of canopy disturbances on fuelbed structure and fire behavior" excerpt: "In this project, we aim to enhance the understanding of how natural canopy disturbances influence fuel dynamics in fire-dependent forests. The objective is to provide forest managers with insights into the impacts of canopy loss on fuel dynamics and subsequent fire behavior, with the potential to leverage these disturbances for fire management strategies, such as in thinning operations. We use LiDAR and canopy height models to detect gap formation by strong winds and hurricanes, followed by surveys of surface fuels and comparisons between disturbed and undisturbed areas. Through prescribed fires, we also assess differences in fire behavior between these areas.
